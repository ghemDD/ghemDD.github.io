SkySpector: Query-Centric Multi-Positive Retrieval for Earth Observation

Oral, TerraBytes II Workshop at ECCV 2026 · Oral, 2nd ESA–NASA Workshop on AI Foundation Models for Earth Observation · Extended manuscript in preparation
N. Ouerghemi, C. Tomoiaga, M. Detyniecki
PDF

Summary

SkySpector is the first multi-positive, query-centric text-to-image retrieval benchmark for Earth observation: 412 analyst-style queries with expert-vetted, pool-wide relevance labels over a shared pool of 972 images, stratified by semantic level (scene, region, instance) and tagged with six visual phenomena. Built during a research internship at AXA.

Key findings

  • Across 16 general-purpose and remote-sensing VLMs, model rankings that are stable on caption benchmarks (Kendall’s τ ≥ 0.72) collapse under analyst-style queries (τ = 0.13), with the largest drops on relation, count, and layout queries.
  • MLLMs used as training-free rerankers recover +13.9 NDCG@10 over the best first-stage retrieval score, implicating single-vector compression as a key retrieval bottleneck.
