Desktop GUI Grounding

Research engineer internship · Logitech

Summary

LogiSpot is a desktop-centric GUI grounding benchmark: 715 screenshot–instruction pairs across 8 professional app domains, icon-heavy (66.4%) with tiny targets (0.10% mean area). It exposes a real domain shift: UI-TARS1.5 7B drops from 49.6% on ScreenSpot-Pro to 28.8% on LogiSpot.

LogiActor is a coordinate-free grounding model built on Qwen2.5VL (3B) with modality-specific actor heads (text vs. icon), trained via attention-map supervision for robust localization in high-resolution UIs.
